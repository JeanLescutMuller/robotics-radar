- Swiss Robotics Association, ETH RobotX, ETH robotics labs and seminars, UZH robotics seminars, DARE Campus, Zürich AI Festival/ecosystem, Hackevents, public LinkedIn events/posts, Women in Robotics Switzerland, Switzerland Innovation Park Zürich, Roboto, Founderful, Zürich Robotics Roundtable, Foxglove Actuate, Meetup, Eventbrite.
